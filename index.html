Pest control

What to request
Full inspection + treatment: termites, cockroaches, ants, mosquitoes. Ask if they treat the garden perimeter.
Typical cost
S$150–350 one-time. Quarterly contracts: ~S$80–150/visit.
Where to find
Rentokil, Killem Pest, Anticimex — NEA-licensed. Also check Carousell for local operators.

Gardening / lawn

What to request
Mowing, trimming hedges, clearing leaves, edging. First visit usually costs more.
Typical cost
S$80–180/visit for a terrace. Monthly contracts cheaper per visit.
Where to find
Helpling, Kaodim, Facebook "Singapore Gardening" groups. Ask neighbours — corner terraces often share a gardener.

Electrician

What to request
Check wiring, install lighting points, set up dimmers. Must be EMA-licensed.
Typical cost
S$80–150/hr or per job. New light point: ~S$80–150 each.
Where to find
Sendhelper, Kaodim, or ask your lighting shop — many have trusted installers they refer.
